Shrink portfolio pictures

Makes small copies of your pictures (150 DPI by default), so the portfolio fits on GitHub and loads fast. Everything happens on your PC, nothing is uploaded. Use Chrome or Edge.

1. Add pictures

Add any of these, as many times as you like: single pictures, a project folder, a sub-folder (like HVAC), or your whole “The Portofolio” folder. The tool knows every picture of the website and puts each one in its right folder.

2. Settings

The DPI is read from each picture (Revit and most programs save it). A picture is only made smaller, never bigger.

Add pictures first.

Download portfolio-pictures.zip Download updated data.js

4. Then

Unzip portfolio-pictures.zip. Inside are the folders Projects PNGs, Certifications and Personal image (only the ones you added), with the exact names the website expects. Upload them to GitHub, or put them next to index.html on your PC.

New, renamed or deleted pictures: when you add folders, the tool also updates the website’s picture list. If something changed, the zip contains assets/js/data.js (also available with the “Download updated data.js” button). Put it in the website’s assets/js folder, replacing the old one. New projects get their folder name as title; add their details in data.js later. Pictures added one by one (without their folder) are not added to the list; they go to a folder called Not on the website yet.
